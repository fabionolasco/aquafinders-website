About Us

AquaFinders is a specialized groundwater location service operating throughout North and South Carolina. With our team of four experienced geologists/hydrogeologists and one forest engineer, we use cutting-edge technology to locate the best spots for drilling productive water wells.

Founded on the principle that everyone deserves access to clean, reliable water, our experts have helped hundreds of homeowners, farmers, and businesses avoid the costly mistake of drilling in the wrong location.

Our scientific approach combines electrical resistivity imaging and geophysics analysis to maximize your chances of finding water.
