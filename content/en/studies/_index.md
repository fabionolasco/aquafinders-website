## How we can help

- Field collection of electrical resistivity data along survey lines
- Coordination with geologists or engineers who interpret the data for your project
- Delivery of data and images in a format your team can use

## What we don't provide

We don't issue environmental compliance determinations, licensed geological or engineering sign-off, or contamination assessments on our own. If your project requires them, they come from the licensed professional on your team.

[Contact us](/contact/) with your project details to see whether we're a good fit.
