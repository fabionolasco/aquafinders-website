## What we collect

- **Information you send us.** When you use the property review or contact form, we receive what you enter: typically your name, email and/or phone, property location, and project details.
- **How you found us.** The forms also send the page you submitted from, the page where you first arrived, the referring website, and campaign tags (such as `utm_source`) if the link you followed had them. These are stored in your browser so they can be included with your request.
- **Website analytics.** We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site, such as pages viewed and buttons clicked. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as approximate location, device and browser type.

## How forms are processed

Form submissions are delivered to us by email through [Web3Forms](https://web3forms.com/). Forms are protected against spam with hCaptcha, which may process information about your device and interaction to check that you're human.

## How we use your information

We use what you send us only to respond to your request, provide and follow up on our services, and understand which pages and channels bring us inquiries. We do not sell your personal information.

## Your choices

- You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.
- You can ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing [support@aquafinders.site](mailto:support@aquafinders.site).
- You can limit analytics cookies in your browser settings or with Google's [Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
