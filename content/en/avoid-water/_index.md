## How it helps

The same resistivity survey used to look for water can show where conditions suggest moisture, clay or fractured rock along a survey line. For builders and contractors, that's extra information to weigh alongside the usual site investigation before digging.

## Who it's for

- Builders and contractors planning foundations, basements or excavation
- Landowners evaluating where to place structures on a property

## Important limits

This survey is **not** a geotechnical investigation and does not replace one. It does not provide engineering design values, foundation recommendations or compliance sign-off. Those need a licensed geotechnical engineer. Our data can be shared with your engineer as supporting information.
