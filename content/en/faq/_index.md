faqGroups:
  - title: "The basics"
    id: "basics"
    faqs:
      - q: "Do you drill wells?"
        a: "No. AquaFinders is independent of drilling companies. We study the subsurface and recommend where to drill; you hire the driller of your choice and share our report with them."
      - q: "Can you guarantee that I'll find water?"
        a: "No. No survey can honestly guarantee water, a flow rate (GPM) or a depth. What we provide is a professional study that shows where conditions look most and least promising. Only drilling and a yield test confirm what a well produces."
      - q: "Do you test water quality?"
        a: "No. Whether water is safe to drink can only be determined by laboratory testing of a water sample after the well is drilled."
      - q: "Who does the work?"
        a: "AquaFinders manages the project and carries out the field data collection. Independent partner geologists interpret the data and prepare the technical conclusions."
      - q: "Where do you work?"
        a: "We're based in York, SC and serve North Carolina, South Carolina, Georgia, Florida, Tennessee, Kentucky and Virginia (and nationwide upon special request). A travel fee applies beyond 20 miles from York, quoted in advance."
  - title: "Cost"
    id: "cost"
    faqs:
      - q: "How much does it cost?"
        a: "Phase 1, the remote Satellite & Geological Study, is $500. Phase 2, the On-Site Geophysical Survey, is $700. A travel fee applies beyond 20 miles from York, SC; brush clearing is quoted if the survey line needs it; state sales tax is not included. See [pricing](/pricing/)."
      - q: "Do I have to buy both phases?"
        a: "Phase 1 is required before Phase 2. After Phase 1, it's your choice whether to continue, and we'll tell you if we don't think the on-site survey is worthwhile."
      - q: "Would I get a refund if the result is negative?"
        a: "No. Finding that a property, or part of it, has low groundwater potential is a valid result of the study, and can save you the cost of a dry hole."
      - q: "Do you offer discounts for larger projects?"
        a: "Projects with several properties or wells can be quoted together. Ask us."
  - title: "The process and the survey"
    id: "process"
    faqs:
      - q: "What is the process?"
        a: "You send a property review request, we discuss scope, Phase 1 studies the property remotely and recommends whether and where to survey, Phase 2 collects data on site, partner geologists interpret it, and you receive a report to share with your driller. See [how it works](/how-it-works/)."
      - q: "When should I hire you?"
        a: "Ideally before you contact a driller, or even before you buy the land. If a permit has already been filed, we can still help, though the permit may need to be amended."
      - q: "How deep can the survey see?"
        a: "It depends on the length of the survey line, the equipment setup and the ground itself, including recent weather. Under favorable conditions, the survey can image several hundred feet; in other conditions, less. The report states what the data supports at your site."
      - q: "What size property does the study cover?"
        a: "The standard study covers properties up to 50 acres; larger properties are quoted individually. Note that the on-site survey produces a vertical cross-section along a straight line, not a map of the whole property, which is why Phase 1 chooses where that line goes."
      - q: "Can the results be inconclusive?"
        a: "Yes. Some sites give clearer signals than others. When the data doesn't support a confident recommendation, the report says so plainly."
      - q: "Can you work with my well driller?"
        a: "Yes. The report is written to be shared with your driller, and we're glad to answer their questions."
  - title: "Other projects"
    id: "other"
    faqs:
      - q: "Do you do surveys for construction, grounding or environmental projects?"
        a: "We can collect resistivity data for some of these projects, working with the qualified professional responsible for the project. Pricing depends on scope. See [avoiding water](/avoid-water/), [resistivity surveys](/resistivity/) or [contact us](/contact/)."
