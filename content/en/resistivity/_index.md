## What we do

AquaFinders carries out field resistivity data collection: laying out survey lines, taking measurements and delivering the data for interpretation. For water wells, interpretation is done by our independent partner geologists. For other projects, we work with the professional responsible for your project.

## Resistivity imaging vs. grounding soil-resistivity tests

These are related but different:

- **Resistivity imaging (ERI/ERT)** produces cross-section images of the subsurface. This is what we use for groundwater studies.
- **Soil-resistivity testing for electrical grounding** (often the Wenner four-pin method) provides values that electrical engineers use to design grounding systems for substations, solar farms and industrial sites.

If your project needs grounding-design data, tell us the specification your engineer requires and we'll confirm whether we can collect it. The grounding design itself must be done by a qualified electrical engineer.
