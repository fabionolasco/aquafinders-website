## Who this is for

- Homeowners and homesteaders planning a **first well** on new or raw land
- Builders who need a dependable water supply before construction moves ahead
- Landowners who want to pick a well location before laying out a home, septic or barns

## What the study can answer, and what it can't

**It can help answer:**

- Which parts of your property look more and less promising for groundwater?
- Where should the driller start, based on the subsurface data?
- Is there anything in the data that suggests a different approach, or extra caution?

**It can't answer:**

- Exactly how many gallons per minute a well will produce
- Whether the water will be safe to drink (that needs a lab test after drilling)
- Whether permits or setbacks allow a well at a given spot (your driller and county handle that)
