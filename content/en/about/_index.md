## What we do

AquaFinders is based in York, South Carolina, and serves North Carolina, South Carolina, Georgia, Florida, Tennessee, Kentucky and Virginia (and nationwide upon special request). We coordinate groundwater studies for people who are about to drill a water well, or who need to drill again because an existing well isn't enough.

## How we work

- **AquaFinders** is your single point of contact. We manage the project, schedule the work and carry out the **field data collection** on your property.
- **Independent partner geologists** process and interpret the data and prepare the technical conclusions in your report.
- **Your well driller**, chosen by you, uses the report to plan the drilling. We don't drill wells and we don't sell drilling services.

This keeps the study independent: our recommendation doesn't depend on who drills, how deep they go or how many wells get drilled.

## What we believe

Drilling a well is a big decision made with limited information. A careful study can improve that information, but it can't remove all uncertainty, so we're upfront about what our results mean and what they don't.

We speak English and Spanish, and we'd rather tell you a study isn't worth it than sell you one you don't need.
