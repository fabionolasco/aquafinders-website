## What the price includes

- Project coordination with one point of contact
- The remote study (Phase 1) and, if you go ahead, the on-site data collection (Phase 2)
- Interpretation by independent partner geologists
- Written reports delivered by email, ready to share with your driller

## What it does not include

- **Drilling.** AquaFinders does not drill wells. Drilling is contracted separately with the driller of your choice.
- **Water testing.** Water quality and safety require laboratory tests after drilling.
- **Permits.** Well permits and setbacks are handled by your driller and local authorities.
- **A guarantee of water, depth or flow.** You're buying a professional study, not a result.
