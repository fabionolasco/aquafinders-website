## What happens next

1. **We review your request** and look at the location.
2. **We contact you** the way you prefer, to ask any questions and explain the options.
3. **You decide.** If a study makes sense, we send the scope and price before any work starts.

## Good to know

- AquaFinders **does not drill wells**. Our study helps you and your driller choose where to drill.
- No survey can guarantee water, a flow rate or a depth.
- Phase 1 is $500 and Phase 2 is $700; travel beyond 20 miles from York, SC and brush clearing are quoted up front. [See pricing](/pricing/).
