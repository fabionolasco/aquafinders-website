## The process in three steps

## Step by step

1. **You send a property review request.** Location, what you need, and anything you already know about the land or existing wells.
2. **We talk.** We confirm the study fits your situation, explain the scope, and quote any travel or clearing fees.
3. **Phase 1, the Satellite & Geological Study ($500).** After the agreement and payment, the property is studied remotely: satellite imagery, terrain, geological maps and available records of wells in the area.
4. **Phase 1 report and recommendation.** You receive a report showing the more and less promising parts of the property, and a clear recommendation on whether an on-site survey is worthwhile and where its line(s) should go.
5. **You decide.** If you continue, you pay for Phase 2.
6. **Phase 2, the On-Site Geophysical Survey ($700).** Our field team collects resistivity data on your property along the chosen line(s).
7. **Interpretation.** Independent partner geologists process and interpret the data.
8. **Final report.** Delivered by email, with the cross-section image(s), recommended drilling location(s) and limitations. Share it with your driller; we're happy to answer their questions.

Timing depends on scheduling, weather and site access. Typical turnaround is about five weeks from agreement to final report; rush service may be available for an extra fee."

## Who does what

AquaFinders manages your project from start to finish and carries out the field data collection. All data analysis, interpretatopm, and technical conclusions are done by independent partner geologists. But rest assured, you will deal only with us throughout the whole process. We got your back!

## What you receive {#deliverables}

- **After Phase 1:** a property map showing areas of higher and lower groundwater potential, the reasoning behind it, and the proposed survey line location(s).
