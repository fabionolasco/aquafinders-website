Process and Pricing

Our services are divided into two parts:

$500
(A) Geological Study

We conduct a comprehensive evaluation of your property that includes the analysis of multiple satellite images, detailed topographic assessments, thorough geological studies, and in-depth hydrogeological investigations. This multi-layered approach enables us to identify the areas with the highest water potential. This study also helps reduce your costs by directing us to the best location for field tests, eliminating the need for multiple expensive tests.

$700
(B) Electrical Resistivity Imaging

We perform a Geophysical Field Survey at your property utilizing Electrical Resistivity Imaging (ERI) technology, followed by comprehensive data analysis, professional interpretation of results (with multiple geologists), detailed report generation, and a clear explanation of the findings to ensure full understanding and practical application. This report will be used by your well driller/builder in their decision-making.

$725
(B.1) Possibility (in some cases)

Properties on a sedimentary geological formation, may require the Schlumberger SEV survey, which is more labor intensive, and therefore requires an additional $725 fee. Contact us for more information.

Note: The Geological Study must always be completed before an ERI survey.

Steps

1
[Customer] You fill out our New Customer Form (Start now)
2
[AquaFinders] We contact you to learn more about your needs
3
[Customer] You sign the contract and make the payment ($500)
4
[AquaFinders] We begin with aquifer characterization and a regional well audit, followed by geospatial, topographical, geological, and hydrogeophysical analyses of your property
5

6

7
[Customer] You make the payment for the second study ($700)
8
[AquaFinders] We perform the geophysical survey on your property
9
[AquaFinders] We deliver the final results to you by e-mail, so you can share it with your well drilling company or builder.

Important notes:

  • If everything goes well (pun-intented), the process can take as little as three weeks.
  • The usual turnaround is about five weeks.
  • We offer rush services for emergency projects (extra fees apply).
  • Please note that the process and steps will vary depending on your needs, preferences, and weather conditions.
  • We offer discounts for larger projects that require multiple studies.
  • State sales taxes are not included in the prices shown on this website.
